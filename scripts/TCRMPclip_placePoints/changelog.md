# TCRMPclip_placePoints — changelog

- 2026-09-27 (LO): load_cpc_points now searches every cpc_all season folder for a pre-2020 frame's points (new helper find_cpc_rows_any_season), not only its calendar-year folder, because a season surveyed late in the year carries dates into the next January through June (cpc_all/2017 holds 2,623 frames dated into 2018); the calendar year wins when a frame's rows differ between seasons, and the choice is logged. A .cpc file indexed for a frame whose raw image is missing no longer drops the frame, since its points always come from point_coords.csv, never from the .cpc file's own geometry. Recovers 57 of run_26-09-18_AT's selected frames that the old calendar-year-only lookup dropped as "no CPC points found." Rationale: fixes a coverage gap found in the 2026-09-26 VEGA training-data audit (see /mnt/rip/vicarius_drive/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-27-vega-annotation-safety-net.md).
- 2026-07-03 (LO): export_batch scales point coords by the selected_frames 'scale' column for lores frames so prompts land in lores image space.
- 2026-07-03 (LO): lores delivery moved to routing. export_batch(lores_mode) delivers every >1920px frame from its deterministic lores twin (path mirror off the resolved raw path, NOT a reconstructed name -> never skips), scaling points by the twins actual/orig long-edge ratio; already-small frames + missing-twin frames pass through as-is. Replaces the selected_frames is_lores/manifest approach.
