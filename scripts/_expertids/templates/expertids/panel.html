1 Publish flagged masks to the expert site

When you flag a point REVIEW (Step 4) it is segmented (Step 5) and published to a public GitHub-Pages site the expert browses. Exports publish automatically, but if a push failed (lab network / no token) the live site can fall behind. This reconciles and pushes it: you see exactly what will change (new masks added, completed IDs removed) before anything goes to GitHub. - item(s) currently pending review.

Reconciles the live site with the current queue, then publishes to GitHub.

2 Send the link to your taxonomic expert

Email this URL to the expert. They label each mask in the browser and export a CSV (uid,code,confidence,reviewer,project_id; the confidence column may be blank and is ignored on import) to email back. The site reflects whatever you last published in Step 1.

Link copied.

Reviewer setup (optional)

Record the contact email answers should return to and any extra candidate codes you want surfaced on the viewer (the "potential IDs" the expert is most likely choosing between). Both are saved to the review site; the email also appears in each request below as the return address.

Per-project email requests

One ready-to-send request per project with masks awaiting review. The text carries the subject line, the review-site link, step-by-step instructions, and the return address; copy it, or open a pre-filled draft with the Email draft button.

3 Import the expert's returned CSV

Drop the CSV the expert emailed back. Each row is folded in as a tentative review, keyed by the reviewer, so multiple experts and projects accumulate, nothing is overwritten or pruned. Rows route to their own project (by the project_id column) so identical frame names across projects never cross-contaminate. Tentative IDs show on the site immediately; the final code is chosen later in the Consensus builder. So far: - accepted (expert), - in the library. Re-drop any time more arrive.

Drag the expert's CSV here, or click to choose a file.

Expected columns: uid,code,confidence,reviewer,project_id (the confidence column may be blank and is ignored; extra columns are ignored too).
