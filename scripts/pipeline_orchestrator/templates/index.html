Why a label sometimes does not reach its target
  • Not enough frames exist. A rare label may appear in fewer frames than the target, so every available frame is taken and the count still falls short.

What is DDP?
DDP is Distributed Data Parallel, PyTorch's native multi-GPU training mode. The trainer turns it on automatically when the device is comma-separated. Each GPU holds a copy of the model, each step's batch is split across the GPUs, and gradients are all-reduced between GPUs every step so the replicas stay in sync.

When it helps: larger models (l or x), higher image sizes (640 and up), and datasets in the thousands of frames, where per-step compute dominates the inter-GPU sync and gives about 1.7x to 1.9x speedup on 2 GPUs.
When it does not: small datasets, small models, or small image sizes, where the sync overhead eats most of the gain (speedup 1.0x to 1.3x) and per-epoch behaviour gets harder to reason about (validation runs on the first GPU only, and the effective batch doubles unless the batch size is halved).

Alternative: pick Single GPU, cuda:1 for this run so the other GPU is free for a second independent experiment in parallel, which is twice the throughput across experiments.
